01 · Profile

About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the International Center for Mathematical Sciences-Sofia, Institute of Mathematics and Informatics, Bulgarian Academy of Sciences. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in 2025 under the supervision of Professor Rinat Kedem.

My research interests are cluster algebra and representation theory. My recent work studies cluster algebras and generalized cluster algebras arising from surfaces and orbifolds, including combinatorial expansion formulas, skein relations, and unimodality phenomena. At FRAME, I also contribute to the collaborative price-surge prediction and systematic-trading projects listed below.

05 · Projects

Ongoing Projects

01

Predicting Price Surges with Neural ODE

Research Paper · 2026

Integrates correlated jump-diffusion and Neural ODE models for binary surge classification on KOSDAQ, IWM, and S&P 500, benchmarked against LSTM, CNN, and Transformer baselines.

02

Price-Surge Prediction for Systematic Trading

Work in progress

Building on our research on price-surge prediction with Neural ODEs, this project develops an end-to-end systematic trading framework that converts predicted surge probabilities into actionable trading signals. We compare Neural ODEs, XGBoost, and other sequence-learning models while studying signal construction, model training, trading rules, portfolio formation, and out-of-sample performance.
