About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My research combines mathematical modeling, economic intuition, and computational methods to study problems in financial markets, risk-management, and data-driven prediction.

05 · Projects

Ongoing Projects

01

Multi-Period Spatio-Temporal Risk Sharing

Work in progress

This project develops a multi-period framework for spatio-temporal risk sharing and characterizes Pareto-optimal allocations and Nash equilibria. It examines risk-sharing arrangements both with and without solvency-capacity constraints and studies how equilibrium allocations change when participants can raise additional reserves at a cost.

02

Price-Surge Prediction for Systematic Trading

Work in progress

Building on our research on price-surge prediction with Neural ODEs, this project develops an end-to-end systematic trading framework that converts predicted surge probabilities into actionable trading signals. We compare Neural ODEs, XGBoost, and other sequence-learning models while studying signal construction, model training, trading rules, portfolio formation, and out-of-sample performance.
