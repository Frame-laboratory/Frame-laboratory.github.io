01 · Profile

About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, specializing in actuarial science and risk analytics. My research combines mathematical modeling, economic reasoning, and computational methods to study problems in risk sharing, insurance, financial markets, and data-driven prediction.

At FRAME, I serve as Research Lead and coordinate interdisciplinary projects involving machine learning, quantitative finance, mathematical modeling, and systematic research. I am particularly interested in developing models that are mathematically rigorous, computationally practical, and economically meaningful.

03 · Research

Research Interests

My research interests span mathematical risk modeling, insurance, financial prediction, and computational methods.

Insurance and Risk

Risk Sharing and Risk Pooling

Equilibrium, welfare, stability, participation incentives, pricing, and reinsurance in heterogeneous insurance systems.

Applied Probability

Stochastic Modeling

Stochastic processes, first-passage events, dynamic risk allocation, and multi-period models under uncertainty.

Financial Markets

Quantitative Finance

Financial time series, asset-price dynamics, market-event prediction, systematic signals, and risk-aware decision making.

Machine Learning

Rare-Event Prediction

Neural ODEs, sequence models, gradient-boosted trees, and classification methods for low-signal and imbalanced data.

Optimization

Allocation and Mechanism Design

Optimization, constrained allocation, auction mechanisms, pricing design, and strategic participation.

Interdisciplinary Research

Mathematics, Economics, and Data

Combining rigorous mathematical analysis with economic interpretation, empirical validation, and computational modeling.

04 · Publications

Selected Papers

01

Predicting Price Surges with Neural ODE

Seongyoon Kim and collaborators

Manuscript under review

Develops a Neural ODE-based framework for predicting whether asset prices cross a substantial return threshold within a fixed horizon under low signal-to-noise and rare-event settings.

02

On the Expansion of Risk Pooling

Joint work with Runhuan Feng and Michalis Anthropelos

Management Science

Studies when expanding a heterogeneous risk pool improves participants' welfare and remains stable, with particular attention to risk-adjusted pricing, participation incentives, and reinsurance.

03

Tokenization of Distributed Insurance by Auction

Joint work with Runhuan Feng and Amichai Painsky

Japanese Journal of Statistics and Data Science

Proposes an auction-based mechanism for allocating tokenized insurance risks and analyzes investor allocation, bidding, portfolio construction, and the effects of splitting and merging bids.
