01 · Profile

About

I am a machine learning researcher with a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My work spans quantitative finance, scientific machine learning, and physics-based simulation, with a focus on building models that are both predictive and computationally practical.

At FRAME, I lead research on predicting abrupt stock market movements by integrating stochastic differential equation models with deep learning approaches. I have also developed forecasting and trading systems across KOSDAQ and S&P 500 datasets, and built differentiable frameworks for coarse-grained molecular dynamics and high-performance multiphysics simulations.
