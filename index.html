About

FRAME (Financial Research in AI, Mathematics, and Economics) is a research team with previous and current affiliations to the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. We combine stochastic modeling and machine learning to study non-stationarity, low signal-to-noise ratios, and the prediction of rare but high-impact market events.

Our work spans theoretical research, such as extending jump-diffusion frameworks and developing continuous-time neural architectures, and applied systems, including ML-driven trading algorithms built end-to-end from data to execution. We believe rigorous modeling and practical engineering go hand in hand.
